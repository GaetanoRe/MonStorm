Demo scene for the Health system.


How to use demo:

A key - damage the health by 10
S key - heal the health by 4
D key - increase the max health by 10
F key - decrease the max health by 10


How to setup:
Add the Health script to any object, set the desired values, and that's it.
For any behavior when the Health gets damaged or dies, use the provided events OnCurrentHealthUpdated and OnDeath.
